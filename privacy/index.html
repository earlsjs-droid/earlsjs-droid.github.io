Kingfisher Run: Golden Hour Edition

Privacy Policy

This game collects no personal data. Nothing you do in it is sent to us.

Effective 19 November 2026

What the game collects

Nothing. Kingfisher Run has no account, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not connect to any server of ours. It plays fully offline.

What stays on your iPhone

Your best score for each chapter and each kind of flight, your journal and field notes, which tips you have already seen, the chapter you last played and your settings (pace, sound, vibration, controls, battery saver) are saved on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. They never leave the device through the game. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Game Center so they can appear on its leaderboards. That information is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us, and we receive no personal information from it. You can sign out of Game Center, or change who sees your profile, in your iPhone's Settings under Game Center. The game works the same whether or not you use Game Center.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children.

Sharing and selling

There is no data to share or sell, and we do neither.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything it saved. To remove your Game Center scores, use Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date before the change takes effect in the game.
