How to play

Drag left and right to steer, up and down to climb or swoop. Tap Dive when the ring on the water lines up over a fish, and Boost to speed up. Your first flight starts with a short guided How to play, and you can replay it from the title screen any time.

Too fast?

Choose Gentle pace on the title screen. The river slows, the catch window widens and the hunters give you more time. Scores still count.

No sound

Check that the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone is not set to silent, and that Sound is on in the pause menu. Music and effects each have their own volume there.

Your progress

Best scores, the journal and settings are saved on your iPhone, inside the app. They stay when you close the app or restart the phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings to post scores to the chapter leaderboards and earn achievements. Anything you earned before signing in is sent the next time you play.

Vibration

Turn it on or off in the pause menu.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
