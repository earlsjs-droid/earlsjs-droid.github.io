Kingfisher Run is an iPhone game by J.S. Earls.

How to play

Drag left and right to steer, up and down to climb or swoop. Tap Dive when your ring on the water lines up over a fish's ring, or just tap quickly on the side where you steer, so you can play with one thumb. Hold Boost to fly faster; wisps refill it. Your first flight starts with a short guided How to play, you can replay it from the title screen any time, and new things are explained the first time you meet them.

Too fast, or too easy?

Choose Gentle on the title screen. The river slows, the catch window widens and the hunters give you more time. Scores still count. Want more of a fight? Choose Wild: no aim assist and no dive prompt, more hunters that feint and stoop fast, and every score is worth half as much again.

More flights

Under More flights on the title screen: Today's river (one river a day, the same for everyone), Second brood (the river loops; keep the chicks fed as long as you can), The year (all four seasons in one flight) and Free flight.

Left-handed, or running warm?

In the pause menu, Controls switches the steering side and the buttons for left-handed play, and Battery saver caps the game at 30 frames a second.

No sound

Check that the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone is not set to silent, and that Sound is on in the pause menu. Music and effects each have their own volume there.

Your progress

Best scores, the journal and its field notes, and settings are saved on your iPhone, inside the app. They stay when you close the app or restart the phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings to post scores to the chapter leaderboards and earn achievements. Anything you earned before signing in is sent the next time you play.

Vibration

Turn it on or off in the pause menu.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data. Read the privacy policy.
